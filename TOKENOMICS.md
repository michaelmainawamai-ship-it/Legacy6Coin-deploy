# Legacy6Coin (L6C) Tokenomics

Legacy6Coin is a capped ERC-20 project focused on transparency, stewardship, documentation, and resilience. The tokenomics framework describes the documented supply structure without making promises about market value, financial returns, exchange listings, or future utility.

## Supply Governance

The contract owner may mint only within the remaining capacity. The contract prevents issuance that would exceed 1,000,000 L6C. Supply controls are enforced by code and should be independently reviewed through the verified contract implementation.

## Burn Policy

Holders may burn their own tokens. Burning reduces circulating supply. Burning does not restore minting capacity or increase the contract's maximum supply. The `totalMinted` value tracks lifetime issuance separately from the supply remaining after burns.

## Witness System

Witnesses receive witness allocations and are publicly recorded on-chain.

Witnesses do not possess:

- Governance powers
- Ownership rights
- Minting rights
- Administrative control

A witness allocation is not represented by this document as conferring any additional contractual or governance authority.
