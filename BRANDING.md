# Legacy6Coin Official Branding

## Brand Identity

**Official Name:** Legacy6Coin

**Ticker:** L6C

**Founder:** Michael Wamai Maina Gatane
**Official Sigil:** Legacy6Coin Flame Sigil

The Legacy6Coin Flame Sigil is the official visual identity of the Legacy6Coin
project. It represents the project's documented connection to legacy,
identity, resilience, and continuity.

### Symbolic Meaning

- **Flame** represents resilience through adversity and the continuation of
  purpose through difficult circumstances.
- **Number 6** identifies the Legacy6 identity and anchors the mark to the
  project's name.
- **L6C** identifies the sigil with Legacy6Coin.
- **Red** represents persistence, courage, and endurance.
- **Legacy** represents remembrance beyond a single moment and preservation
  for future generations.

## Official Assets

The original and derived branding assets are archived in the repository:

- [Original JPG](./assets/L6C-Sigil.jpg)
- [Portable PNG](./assets/L6C-Sigil.png)
- [Vector SVG](./assets/L6C-Logo.svg)

The JPG is the archival master supplied for the official sigil. The PNG is a
direct portable representation. The SVG is a scalable project identity asset
and should be used for large-format or high-resolution applications.

## Usage Guidelines

- Use the official assets without changing the sigil, number, wordmark,
  proportions, or core red color.
- Preserve clear space around the mark so that it remains visually distinct.
- Do not add financial claims, promotional slogans, price information, or
  misleading project statements to the sigil.
- Do not combine the sigil with third-party brands in a way that implies
  endorsement or affiliation.
- Use the vector SVG for scalable layouts and the PNG or JPG for fixed-size
  digital or archival uses.
- If any use requires a modified adaptation, retain the original asset unchanged
  and document the adaptation separately.

## Attribution

The Legacy6Coin Flame Sigil is attributed to the Legacy6Coin project and
Founder Michael Wamai Maina Gatane. Uses associated with the project should
identify that attribution while preserving the original artwork.

Contributors may propose asset changes for archival review, but the official
asset should not be silently replaced or altered.

## Preservation Commitment

The official branding assets are treated as permanent project artifacts and
historical records. Preservation work should retain:

- the original supplied image;
- unchanged PNG and SVG derivatives;
- source and checksum records;
- IPFS CID references when they become available; and
- a clear record of superseded or retired assets.

See [IPFS Preservation Plan](./IPFS_PRESERVATION_PLAN.md) for archival
procedures and CID placeholders.
